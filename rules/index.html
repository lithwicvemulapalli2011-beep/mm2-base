Rules & Safety

These guidelines are for community safety and public verification. They are not legal advice and do not promise absolute protection from scams.

Server Rules

  • Respect all members and staff.
  • Use official channels and public verification tools.
  • Do not pressure anyone into urgent trades.
  • Report impersonation or suspicious behavior.

MM Terms of Service

  • Middlemen should communicate clearly and honestly.
  • Public information should match the actual Discord identity.
  • Disputes should be handled through official community channels.
  • Members should confirm all terms before a trade starts.

Scam Policy

  • Verify the exact Discord ID before starting any trade.
  • Never trust unsolicited DMs or copied profile names.
  • Do not rely on display names alone to confirm identity.
  • Report suspicious impersonation immediately and share context cautiously.
