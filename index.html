FAQ

A middleman is a trusted community member who helps facilitate trades in a more secure or structured way than direct player-to-player exchange.

Usually, the middleman coordinates the trade process, verifies the terms, and helps keep the exchange organized. The process should always be checked against the exact Discord ID and the community’s public records.

Public membership is checked through the directory and community data. Verification is a trust signal, not a guarantee of risk-free service.

Fees vary by trade and individual service arrangement. Community context and public records should be reviewed before agreeing to any terms.

Use the public lookup tool and confirm the exact Discord ID matches the person you are dealing with. Do not rely on display names or copied usernames.

Report the impersonation through official channels and verify the exact Discord ID before continuing. Community members should avoid trusting unsolicited DMs or renamed profiles.
